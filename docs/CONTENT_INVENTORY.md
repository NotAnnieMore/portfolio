### ALS thesis and replication work

- Main repository: https://github.com/NotAnnieMore/ALSThesis
- Replication repository: https://github.com/NotAnnieMore/ThesisReplication
- Observed material: Python, Jupyter notebooks, TeX, data folders, source/scripts, outputs, models, replication material, and thesis documents.
- Portfolio treatment: one academic/research case study rather than two repository-shaped entries.
- Verified scope: two connected PRO-ACT studies covering functional progression
  classification and survival prognosis replication/extension.
- Portfolio treatment: full bilingual case study centred on validation design,
  patient-level separation, class imbalance, decision thresholds, held-out
  evaluation, uncertainty, and cautious interpretation of SHAP/LIME evidence.
- Results shown: a focused selection of held-out metrics with their trade-offs;
  no clinical deployment or model-superiority claim is made.
- Media: four optimised WebP figures generated by the thesis pipelines, covering
  the experimental workflow, Precision–Recall evaluation, and SHAP analysis for
  both studies.
- Links: both public repositories are shown.
